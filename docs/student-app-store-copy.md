## Short description

Practice languages and complete lessons assigned by your teacher.

## Full description

Take your language learning with you.

Eluency connects students to the lessons and tests assigned by their teacher.
Enter your six-character access code to open your study workspace.

- Find your assigned lessons and tests.
- Practice with typing, multiple-choice, listening, and image activities.
- Track your learning progress and achievements.
- Download lessons for offline practice.
- Receive assignment reminders and notifications when test results are reviewed.
- See your teacher's instructions and feedback.

Your teacher manages your assignments through Eluency on the web. Ask them for
your student access code before using the app.

Internet access is needed for initial sign-in, loading new assignments, and
syncing learning results. Downloaded lessons support offline practice.
